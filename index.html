METHODOLOGY

How the analysis works

01

Acquire

Load a real recording and read its sampling frequency and duration.

02

Prepare

Combine stereo channels to mono, select a short segment, and remove its mean value.

03

Transform

Apply a Hann window and compute the discrete Fourier spectrum using an FFT algorithm.

04

Interpret

Compare waveform shape, spectral peaks, RMS amplitude, and zero-crossing rate without claiming untrained AI accuracy.

Discrete Fourier Transform

X[k] = Σ x[n] e−j2πkn/N

FFT is an efficient algorithm for computing the DFT. The frequency represented by bin k is fk = kfs/N.

LAPLACE TRANSFORM LINK

First-order low-pass filter

Adjust the cutoff frequency to see the theoretical magnitude response. This is a filter-response demonstration, not a claim that the original audio has been denoised.

Transfer function

H(s) = ωc / (s + ωc)

For a first-order RC low-pass filter, ωc = 1/RC and fc = 1/(2πRC). Its magnitude is approximately −3 dB at the cutoff frequency.

APPLICATIONS

Why this matters

Wildlife monitoring

Acoustic recordings can help researchers study animal activity and calls.

Bioacoustics

Frequency and temporal patterns can be compared across recordings.

Audio systems

Filtering can attenuate some frequency ranges; the effect depends on the filter design.
